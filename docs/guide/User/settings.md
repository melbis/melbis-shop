# Settings

The "Settings" section (the "Structure" tab) provides access to **custom
settings** of the store: key variables that a developer creates
individually for a specific store.

On the left is the **"Settings Catalog"** (tree-structured), on the right are **"Settings and Their
Values"** (Name, Value) and **"Description"**. Here you or your employees
change specific key parameters of the store. Access rights to settings can be
configured precisely — so that a specific option can only be changed by a particular
employee.

The settings themselves are created by the developer in the "Development" section → "Settings Registry"
→ "Custom Settings" (see "[Settings Registry](registry.md)").
