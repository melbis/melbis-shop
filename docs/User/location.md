# Location

The "Location" section is for moving products between sections of the store catalog. The same
can be done in the "[Prices](prices.md)" section, but not in
"[Descriptions](descriptions.md)": this allows giving content managers (e.g.,
freelancers) access to arranging products across sections without exposing commercial or
descriptive fields.

## Working with Products

The window is structured like a product section: on the left — **"Store Catalog"**, on the right —
**"Products"**. Loading products, catalog and display modes, local filter,
built-in web module, moving and copying, saving order — these are common
mechanics described in "[Basic Operating Principles](basics.md)".

The operations available here are specifically layout operations: transferring and copying products between
sections (drag and drop; with `Ctrl` — copying), removing a product from a section,
sorting (**"Save Order"**), and quick editing of the section tree on the left.
Commercial and descriptive fields of the product are not edited in this section.
